THE STORY

About The Sopranos

The Sopranos is a television series about Tony Soprano, his family and his life in New Jersey.

The story follows Tony as he tries to balance his family life and his work.

Created by David Chase, the show became one of the most influential television dramas of its era.

THE SERIES

Drama • Crime • Family

A story about power, relationships, loyalty and the difficult balance between family and professional life.
